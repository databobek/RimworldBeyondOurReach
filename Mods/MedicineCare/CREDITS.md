# Credits

`Assemblies/BORMedicineCare.dll` (source: `1.7/SourceMedicine/`) is inspired by **Mod Medicine Patch** by **Antaios**
and its contributors: https://github.com/Antaios/Mod-Medicine-Patch

The idea of giving every modded medicine its own medical care level, by extending `MedicalCareCategory`
beyond its vanilla values, comes from that mod. The code here is a separate reimplementation, written without
copying the original's source. It only loads when Mod Medicine Patch is not active.

Thank you to Antaios and everyone who has kept Mod Medicine Patch going over the years.
