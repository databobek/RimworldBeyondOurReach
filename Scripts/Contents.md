# Contents

## Items
### Solar Weapons
BOR:R adds *ballistic*, *gauss* and *beam* weapons for Solar Tier of technology, each having its own distinct quirks. There are 7 gun types, all filling its own role.
They are not only better than vanilla variants of similar weaponry, but additionally offer a consistent art-style, stylized towards Solarite society.

When loaded with my other mod - *Melee Weapons for All Tech Levels*, there are additional 6 melee weapons dedicated to BOR:R Solar Tier. 

When loaded with *Guns For All Tech Levels*, Solar weapons are reinforced with Solar-tier Charge weapon line-up.
### Kwazaari Weapons
Kwazaari tier of technology offers *ballistic*, *bolters* and *stun* weapons.
When loaded with my other mod - *Melee Weapons for All Tech Levels*, there are additional 6 melee weapons dedicated to BOR:R Kwazaari Tier. 

When loaded with *Guns For All Tech Levels*, Solar weapons are reinforced with Kwazaari-tier Charge weapon line-up.
When loaded with my other mod - *Melee Weapons for All Tech Levels*, there are additional 6 melee weapons dedicated to BOR:R Tenebral Tier. 

### Tenebral Weapons
Tenebral ranged weaponry is limited to one set of guns which is aimed at maiming and inflicting pain
### Solar Materials
Solar Tier grants you access to Sunsteel and Sunsteel Weave, which are better than Plasteel and Hyperweave.

### Kwazaari Materials
Kwazaari Tier grants you access to Quasar Alloy and Quasar Alloy Weave, which are even better than Sunsteel and Sunweave.

### Tenebral Materials
Tenebral Tier grants you access to Tenebrite, the ultimate material filling the role of both a metal and a textile. Crafting it comes at a great price, as it requires you to get human hearts as ingredients.

### Solar Armors
Solar-tier grants access to:
* Solar Marine Armor, an improvement over the normal Marine Armor
* Solar Recon Armor, a lighter armor, an improvement over the recon armor, granting speed bonus
* Solar Daedalus Armor, a light armor with a jetpack.

### Kwazaari Armors
Kwazaari-tier grants access to:
* Kwazaari Marine Armor, an improvement even over the Solar one
* Kwazaari Recon Armor, another iterative improvement
* Kwazaari Mechlord Armor, a greatly improved version of the base Mechlord Armor
* Badzaari Heavy Armor, a heavy outfit slowing you down a lot in exchange for a tremendous protection level.

### Tenebral Armors
Tenebral tier grants access to:
* Tenebral Marine Armor, the ultimate armor of the mod
* Tenebral Machine Lord Armor, the greatest achievement in the mechanitor technology.

## Buildings
### Solar Buildings
Full set of improved production, extraction and recreation buildings; especially interesting being the Neutronium extractors,
allowing to construct pseudo-replicating machines and Crust drillers that let you extract minerals.
### Kwazaari Buildings
More of the same, with powerful railgun turrets, letting you erase settlements with proper mods
### Tenebral Buildings
The last of the scaling tiers of buildings, with pain-inducing turrets and fuel-less reactor.
### Umbral Buildings
Technology truly beyond the reach of normal humans - reactors of infinite power, replicators not requiring inputs to put out material.

## Factions
### Solar Republics
An ultra-tech faction that is quite friendly to you - think hi-tech outlanders. They will gladly trade with you, and they have stuff to seel - but they set the prices.
### Kwazaar Bazaar
An ultra-tech faction beyond the Solarite-level. They will only trade with people they know (same system as Broken Empire). They will typically have fewer settlements with lots of things to trade, but annoyingly expensive.
### Badzaari
The bad boys wielding the Kwazaar technology. They bear implants and heavy armour and they come for your packaged survival meals.
### Tenebral Vanquishers
Tenebral Vanquishers, the most dangerous hunters in the Rim. They want to eat your face
## Xenotypes
### Solarite

### Kwazaari
A human that is kinda smarter but even more reliant on technology.
## Permits
### Kwazaari permits
Getting the trust (Scrip) of the Kwazaari faction by doing stuff for them, letting you make drops of artifacts, resources and orbital bombardments.
## Bionics
Installing the mods bionics requires not only the necessary part and mods' medciine of appropriate level, but also some small machines to install it. These bionics are too complex to be installed by pesky monkeys.
### Solar Bionics
Solar Bionics are improved versions of the bionics you already know, costing a bit more and requiring nano-clusters, in return for increased effectiveness.
### Kwazaari Bionics
Kwazaari bionics are new bionics. Installing them requires kwazaari medicine and picoclusters. They are more complex and they combine the effects of a couple old-style bionics into one, allowing you to improve more while wielding less.
### Tenebral Bionics
Tenebral bionics are oddities. Self-forming organs or appendages, combining whole new capabilities in one part. That requires femtoclusters and the best medicine.
### Umbral Bionics
Similarly to solar ones, they are more straight reproductions. Installing them requires attoclusters, and uncraftable resource, that makes organ or appendages reform in new and unique ways.
