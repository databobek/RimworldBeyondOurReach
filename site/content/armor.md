Each tier has a heavy marine set and a bodysuit as its ultimate piece of clothing, and Solar and Kwazaari add a lighter, faster set. Some highlights:

- **Solar marine armor**, an improved marine armor inspired by Mass Effect's N7.
- **Solar daedalus armor**, a light jump-pack armor that comes with Royalty, inspired by XCOM's Archangel.
- **Kwazaari marine armor**, even tougher, inspired by Helldivers 2.
- **Kwazaari machine lord suit**, an upgrade to Biotech's mechlord armor, inspired by Starship Troopers.
- **Tenebral power armor**, the last step up from Kwazaari marine armor.

The flak jackets, assembly harness and carapace double as work gear for bionic crafting and neutronium forging.
