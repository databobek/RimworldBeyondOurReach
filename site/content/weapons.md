Each tier has its own line of guns, each a clear step above the last:

- **<span class="t-solar">Solar</span>**: ballistic and gauss guns.
- **<span class="t-kwazaari">Kwazaari</span>**: ballistic guns and bolt-firing gauss guns.
- **<span class="t-badzaari">Badzaari</span>**: crude guns built from scrap that still hit hard.
- **<span class="t-tenebral">Tenebral</span>**: the strongest guns you can make, built to maim.

With Combat Extended, Solar and Kwazaari also get less-lethal beam weapons that stun instead of kill, and the Solar, Kwazaari, Badzaari and Tenebral guns each use their own ammo.

Use the switch at the top to see vanilla or Combat Extended stats. The numbers are for normal quality. Hover over a column header to see what it means, or over a weapon's name for its description.
