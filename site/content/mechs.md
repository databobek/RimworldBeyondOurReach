## Helios mechsuit

*Needs Coventech Mechsuit Framework.*

{{ portrait("BOR_Helios", "Shown with a rotary gun arm, a cannon arm and a rocket pod.") }}

A Solarite walking fortress. A single pilot rides inside a sunsteel frame between two armored shoulders, each an ablative plate that recharges once it breaks. The arms are interchangeable weapon mounts (rotary gun, cannon, twin cannon or flamethrower) and the back takes a rocket pod.

The reactor burns solarite fuel rods carried in the mech's inventory. Without them it has no power and stands where it is.

{{ screenshot("helios", "The Helios mechsuit") }}

## Kwazaari combat truck

*Needs Vehicle Framework.*

{{ portrait("BOR_KwazaariCombatTruck") }}

A Kwazaari military hauler and the biggest freight vehicle that can still fight. A turret on the cab roof covers every direction, and two fixed cannons along the flanks fire straight ahead. It runs on neutronium and carries a driver, a gunner, a cannoneer and four passengers.

{{ screenshot("combat-truck", "The Kwazaari combat truck") }}

## Umbral harbinger

*Needs Odyssey or Vehicle Framework.*

{{ portrait("BOR_UmbralHarbinger", facing="east") }}

A crescent-winged Umbral craft. It lifts straight up, needs no fuel, and outruns every other aircraft, but it has little room inside. With Odyssey it works as a shuttle that can reach orbit.
