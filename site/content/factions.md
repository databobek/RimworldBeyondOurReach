Every tier of technology has people who already live with it. A few of them will trade with you.

## Solar Republic

{{ faction("BOR_SolarRepublic") }}

Near-ultratech colonizers with a peculiar link to the past: think high-tech outlanders. The Solar Republic starts out neutral, like any outlander faction, and you can win it over. Their caravans visit with Solar-tier goods, and their settlements trade with you, but they set the prices.

With Biotech, Solarites are their own xenotype: a little smarter than baseline humans, and more reliant on technology.

{{ screenshot("faction-solar", "A Solar Republic trade caravan") }}

## Kwazaar Bazaar

{{ faction("BOR_KwazaarBazaar") }}

The Kwazaari are an ultratech power a step beyond the Solarites. They keep no settlements on the map, never turn hostile and never raid. The only way to deal with them is through your comm console.

You sell them ultratech items, or anything better, from your home area, and they pay you in scrip. Scrip buys favors, and each favor has a cooldown before you can call it again:

{{ kwazaari_favors() }}

With Biotech, the Kwazaari are a xenotype too: smarter still, and even more dependent on their machines.

{{ screenshot("faction-kwazaari", "The Kwazaari console with scrip and favors") }}

## Badzaari raiders

{{ faction("BOR_BadzaarHazaard") }}

{{ banner("badzaari", "") }}

Badzaari are what happens when Kwazaari technology ends up in the wrong hands. They wear heavy plate and Kwazaari implants, carry guns built from scrap, and raid you for anything they can carry off.

## Tenebral vanquishers

{{ faction("BOR_Tenebrites") }}

The most dangerous hunters on the Rim. They come in crystalline armor, full of tenebral bionics that make them crave killing, and they have no interest in talking.

{{ screenshot("faction-tenebral", "A Tenebral raid") }}

## Umbrals

> Changed beyond what they once were, they left every banner behind. They hold no settlements and answer no envoys.

Nothing more to say here without spoiling it. See [the Tenebral road](tenebral-road.html).

You won't meet **Orientis** out on the map. It's what your own colony becomes if it takes the Path of Light at the end of [the Tenebral road](tenebral-road.html). The light burns out every tenebral part and kills every Umbral in the colony, and the colonists it cleanses become Orientis.

Orientis colonists can't take bionics. They meditate to make the power their buildings run on, summon those buildings whole instead of building them, and bless their bodies in place of implants. In battle they're crusaders, with glowing power weapons, charge guns and plate.

With Biotech, Orientis is a xenotype. The [Orientis page](orientis.html) has the rest.
