## What the mod does

Beyond Our Reach :: Redux picks up where vanilla research ends. A colony that has finished the vanilla tree can keep climbing through four more tiers of technology:

- **<span class="t-solar">Solar</span>**, which opens up after you research the Kylar-Xar effect computer.
- **<span class="t-kwazaari">Kwazaari</span>**, which needs research and artifacts you analyze.
- **<span class="t-tenebral">Tenebral</span>**, which you don't research so much as bargain for.
- **<span class="t-umbral">Umbral</span>**, technology truly beyond your reach.

Each tier brings its own materials, production buildings, weapons, armor, bionics and drugs, and the factions that already use it.

## What you need

{{ requirements() }}

Combat Extended is optional and fully supported. Guns, turrets and armor have their own CE stats, and the stat pages here have a switch to show either version.

Don't load Beyond Our Reach :: Redux together with the original Beyond Our Reach or with Beyond the Ambition of the Cosmic. Redux replaces both.

## Works with

Beyond Our Reach supports the Royalty, Ideology, Biotech and Odyssey DLCs and a long list of other mods. See [DLCs & mods](integrations.html) for what each one adds.
