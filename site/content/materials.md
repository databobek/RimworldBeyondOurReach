## Neutronium

{{ banner("neutronium", "") }}

Neutronium sits deep underground, like deepchem. Condensers pull it up, pipes carry it, and tanks store it. Tanks need power the whole time: an unpowered tank damages itself until it explodes, so a blackout gets you fireworks.

Neutronium is the explosive in BOR's shells and special Combat Extended ammo, a fuel for power plants, and the raw material of the forges.

## Forges

Forges copy items. A colonist feeds an item to the forge to analyze, which destroys it and teaches the forge a schematic. From then on the forge can make that item over and over from neutronium, without any of the usual ingredients. A healer mech serum, for example, needs nothing but neutronium.

Each schematic can run a set number of times, keep a stock topped up to a target, or run forever. Overdrive multiplies the output, at the cost of more power and wear.
