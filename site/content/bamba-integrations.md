Beyond Our Reach has a handful of sibling mods from the same author. None of them is needed apart from Resource Generator Framework, but they were built together, and each one does more when BOR is loaded.

The Armors, Guns, Melee and Turrets mods all fit the same ladder: Industrial, Spacer, **Solar**, Ultra, **Kwazaari**, **Tenebral**. Solar and Kwazaari are BOR's tiers, and the siblings fill the gaps so you never go straight from a vanilla gun to a Solar one. Each mod is balanced around BOR, so a colony that climbs through the tech levels in order gets a steady rise in power, with no jump to gear that's too strong for where it is.

## Resource Generator Framework

{{ banner("resource-generator", "") }}

[Resource Generator Framework](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3768286295) adds nothing by itself. It's the engine behind BOR's resource drills: how long a drill takes, how many workers it needs and what it produces are all set per building. It's the one mod in this list you have to install.

## Armors For All Post-Medieval Tech Levels

[Armors For All Tech Levels](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3787547731) fills the armor gap between vanilla's tiers. With BOR loaded it does two more things: armor from the Solar, Kwazaari and Orientis lines gets active abilities, and the mod's own armors get [Solarite and Orientis styles](integrations.html#ideology) with Ideology.

Each ability draws on an energy pool that belongs to the armor and refills over about 200 seconds. Pools grow with the tier and the weight class of the armor, so a heavy Kwazaari suit carries the biggest reserve and a light Solar one the smallest. Abilities from the armor you wear show up next to the pawn's other abilities.

## Guns For All Post-Medieval Tech Levels

{{ banner("guns-for-all-tech-levels", "") }}

[Guns For All Tech Levels](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3782391721) adds Spacer and Ultra guns between the vanilla tiers. With BOR it adds Solar and Kwazaari charge guns: a pistol, bullpup, battle rifle, DMR, shotgun, LMG and sniper rifle in each tier. They're made at BOR's benches from sunsteel or Kwazaari alloy, and Ultra guns slot in after Solar in the research order.

With Combat Extended the charge guns get their own ammo. Flip the switch at the top to see both sets of numbers.

## Melee Weapons For All Post-Medieval Tech Levels

{{ banner("melee-for-all-tech-levels", "") }}

[Melee Weapons For All Tech Levels](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3726524693) does the same for blades and hammers. With BOR you get a full set in Solar, Kwazaari and Tenebral: knife, gladius, longsword, hammer, polehammer, mace, baton and spear, plus a shield for each tier with Combat Extended. It also gives the Kwazaari a new favor, an anti-mech squad of four mechbreakers armed with EMP maces.

## Turrets For All Post-Medieval Tech Levels

[Turrets For All Tech Levels](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3800286808) has no content of its own for BOR, but it fits the same ladder, and there's one mechanic worth knowing about.

BOR's factions arrive in drop pods, and anti-air won't shoot down just anything. Ordinary anti-air, which means the point-defense turrets from Combat Extended and from Vanilla Furniture Expanded - Security, can only down Solar pods. A Kwazaari or Badzaari pod shrugs the shot off with a floating "Deflected". BOR's own anti-air, the Solar defender, is a tier higher and reaches up to the Kwazaari. Tenebral and Umbral pods are out of reach of every anti-air turret for now.

The charge AA turret from Turrets For All Tech Levels counts as BOR anti-air too. With Combat Extended loaded, it can down everything the Solar defender can.

## Vanilla Chemfuel Expanded - Unofficial Expansion

[Vanilla Chemfuel Expanded - Unofficial Expansion](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3231387549) adds nothing for BOR directly, but the two go well together. Its raw plasteel refinery turns deepchem into plasteel, and plasteel is what you forge sunsteel from, the metal that Solar technology is built on. So a deepchem field can feed a whole Solar colony.

## Tech Leveler

[Tech Leveler](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3828217997) gives your colony a tech level of its own that moves up as you finish key research. With BOR it knows the mod's tiers: Solar sits before Ultra, Kwazaari after it, and Tenebral and Umbral share the archotech band. A colony that has finished the Solar research is actually Solar, not just a vanilla colony with some Solar equipment.

Research also costs more when you reach for something ahead of your tier, by half the base cost for every tier you skip. That keeps a Kwazaari project from being cheap just because you can afford the materials.
