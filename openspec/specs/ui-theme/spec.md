# UI Theme Specification

## Purpose
BOR's custom windows and inspector tabs (Forge, Dark Altar, Kwazaari console, sale and favors, Tenebral Pod, Blessing, Orientis Manifest) are drawn through a swappable skin, so they can match [LFS] UI Rethemed when it is installed and keep the vanilla look when it is not.

## Requirements

### Requirement: Skin
The windows and tabs SHALL draw their frames, buttons, rows, cells, bars and checkboxes through `BorSkin.Current`, deriving from `BorWindow` and `BorTab`. `BorSkin` itself SHALL be the vanilla look, unchanged from before the skin existed except that sale rows with an amount chosen and the Forge's Clear column button while picking a column are drawn as selected.

### Requirement: UI Rethemed look
When `LazyFridayStudio.UIRethemed.Core` is active, `Mods/UIRethemed` (BORUIRethemed.dll, compiled against UIRethemedCore.dll and loaded only through LoadFolders.xml) SHALL replace the skin with one drawn with that mod's widgets. Windows SHALL use its popup frame (rounded background, title bar, header close button, no vanilla background, shadow, margin or close X); tabs SHALL use its inspector tab frame, with the vanilla tab background switched off for BOR tabs only.

#### Scenario: Theme not installed
- WHEN UI Rethemed Core is not active
- THEN no BOR code references it and every window and tab looks as before

#### Scenario: Theme breaks after an update
- WHEN a themed draw throws from the skin's assembly or UIRethemedCore, or with a missing type or member
- THEN the skin SHALL switch back to vanilla for the session with one warning, and open windows SHALL get their vanilla flags back on the next frame
- AND exceptions from BOR's own window code SHALL NOT switch the theme off

### Requirement: Colour schemes
Schemes SHALL be `BeyondOurReach.BorUISchemeDef` defs (Common/Defs/UI/BOR_UISchemes.xml): hex colours for UI Rethemed's palette slots (well, card, panel, window, titleBar, titleAccent, primary, info, warn, danger, neutral, text, textDim; any left out keep the player's colour), an optional `pattern` (one of UI Rethemed's styles: Honeycomb, Glow, Lines, Crosshatch, Grid, Dots, Stars, Plain) and an optional `patternTexture` with `patternTileSize` (a seamless white-on-transparent mask, made from the tier's floor texture), which with `patternFrames` > 0 names an animated pattern: frames `patternTexture_00`, `_01`, ... looping at `patternFps`, held on the first frame when the theme's animations are off. A building names its scheme with a `BeyondOurReach.BorUIScheme` mod extension (`<scheme>`); a BorWindow names its own in code.

Under the UI Rethemed skin, while a schemed tab or window draws, UI Rethemed's palette SHALL be the player's palette recoloured with the scheme, its pattern the scheme's (through ThemeLook.Previewing), and with a pattern texture every pattern the theme draws SHALL use it as its tile (Harmony prefixes on the internal Patterns.Tile and Patterns.TileSize). All of it SHALL be restored after, also when drawing throws. The vanilla look SHALL ignore schemes. BOR's own painted colours (Forge progress and format overlays, overdrive handle, Altar progress) SHALL follow the active palette's accents under the theme.

#### Scenario: Tabs BOR does not draw
- WHEN an inspector tab that is not a BorTab is drawn for a building with a scheme (Resource Generator Framework's drill tab, the vanilla bills tab)
- THEN its window function SHALL run inside the scheme
- AND the bills tab SHALL also get the theme's tab frame (title bar, border, close button; the shared ITab_Bills is made taller by the title bar only on such a bench) and, while it draws, vanilla text buttons and alternating row backgrounds SHALL be drawn with the theme (Add bill as the main action). The bills code itself SHALL NOT be replaced.

#### Scenario: UI Rethemed internals change
- WHEN the palette or pattern can no longer be swapped
- THEN schemes SHALL be switched off for the session with one warning, and the theme itself SHALL keep working; a missing pattern texture SHALL fall back to the scheme's `pattern`

### Requirement: Tier schemes
- Solar (`BOR_UIScheme_Solar`): gold, light orange and teal from the sunforge, sunsteel tile pattern. Sunforge, crust driller, sunsteel shaper, solar medical fabricator.
- Kwazaari (`BOR_UIScheme_Kwazaari`): periwinkle, silver, orange-red and yellow from tier 2 machines and the kwazaari tile, kwazaari tile line-art pattern. Theta forge, kwazaari crust driller, Kwazifab, kwazaari medical fabricator, and the Kwazaari console, sale and favor windows.
- Orientis (`BOR_UIScheme_Orientis`): gold over deep green, emerald as the info colour, from their buildings; no floor, so the pattern is faint rising flames (24 seamless looping frames generated by Scripts/ui_pattern_flames.py). Blessing and Manifest windows, holy forge, offerings bench.
- Tenebral (`BOR_UIScheme_Tenebral`), shared by Tenebral and Umbral: crimson over near-black with slate, tenebrite tile hexagon pattern. Tenebral crust driller and mine, dark archotech altar, lesser umbral and umbral forges, umbral fabricator, umbral medical fabricator, and the Tenebral Pod window.

### Requirement: Load order
The skin assembly SHALL keep no UI Rethemed type in a field, so that it loads whether or not UI Rethemed is loaded before BOR.

### Requirement: Gizmos
Gizmos (overdrive threshold, meditation power, tenebral psychosis) are out of scope and keep the vanilla look.
